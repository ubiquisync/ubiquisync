## Why Ubiquisync

<FeatureGrid>
	<Feature title="Bring your own storage" icon="cloud-check" accent="#06B6D4">
		Sync over Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or any commodity cloud
		storage — or a dedicated sync server if you prefer. No infrastructure to
		stand up.
	</Feature>
	<Feature title="Conflict-free merging" icon="git-merge" accent="#8B5CF6">
		Per-device append-only logs stamped with hybrid logical clocks merge
		deterministically — structured data and collaborative rich text alike.
	</Feature>
	<Feature title="SQLite or Postgres" icon="database" accent="#3B82F6">
		Store and query your data in the database you already use, with support
		for user-defined schemas à la AirTable or Notion.
	</Feature>
	<Feature title="Local-first and offline" icon="laptop" accent="#10B981">
		Your data lives on-device with full revision history and reactive
		updates. Sync happens whenever connectivity allows.
	</Feature>
</FeatureGrid>
