## Why Ubiquisync

<FeatureGrid>
	<Feature title="Conflict-free by design" icon="git-merge" accent="#8B5CF6">
		Each device writes only to its own append-only log — no device ever
		touches another's files, so file-level conflicts can't exist. Data merges
		deterministically by hybrid logical clock.
	</Feature>
	<Feature title="User-defined schemas" icon="table" accent="#6366F1">
		Let users shape their own tables and columns at runtime, à la AirTable or
		Notion. Schema changes travel through the log and merge conflict-free
		like any other change.
	</Feature>
	<Feature title="SQLite or Postgres" icon="database" accent="#3B82F6">
		Store and query your data in the database you already use — embedded
		SQLite on device, or Postgres.
	</Feature>
	<Feature title="Bring your own storage" icon="cloud-check" accent="#06B6D4">
		Sync over Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or any shared folder — or
		add a dedicated sync server for real-time collaboration and user
		management.
	</Feature>
	<Feature title="Full history and attribution" icon="clock-counter-clockwise" accent="#14B8A6">
		The log is the source of truth: every change is kept, timestamped, and
		attributed to the device that made it. Full revision history comes for
		free.
	</Feature>
	<Feature title="Local-first and offline" icon="laptop" accent="#10B981">
		Your data lives on-device and works offline. Reactive queries update your
		UI the moment local or remote changes land.
	</Feature>
</FeatureGrid>
